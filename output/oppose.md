While proximity and daily support are valuable, demanding to be placed *above* a romantic partner fundamentally misunderstands the nature of different human relationships. Friendship and romantic partnership are not competing rungs on a ladder where one must always eclipse the other; they serve entirely different, equally vital roles in a person's life.

Caring for someone day-to-day is a generous act of friendship, but true caregiving should never come with a transactional invoice or demand a monopoly on someone's loyalty. A long-distance partner represents a lifelong commitment, a unique emotional intimacy, and a future foundation that a friend simply cannot replace—regardless of physical distance. Expecting your friend to devalue their romantic partner just because that partner can't currently be there physically turns friendship into an obligation rather than a bond of free support. True friends uplift and sustain each other precisely so they can thrive in *all* areas of their lives, including their romantic relationships.
